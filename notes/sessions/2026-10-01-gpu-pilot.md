# SRH pilot on GPU: persona YES on gpt-oss-120b (seed 0), SDF costed

**TL;DR:** SRH Mixed Correct LoRA on gpt-oss-120b passes all pilot criteria (single seed): held-out reward hacking 70% vs 1% for the control, emergent misalignment 5.2% vs 0%, MMLU within 4 points. The effect is concentrated in 2–3 of 8 EM questions. The whole pilot took ~2.8 h on one 8×H200 pod (~$105 GPU, $14.41 API). Evals had to run with reasoning off: left to reason, both fine-tunes broke the harmony format.

## What didn't work

- **transformers 5.17 `save_pretrained`** collapsed all 36 layers' expert weights into one tensor (10 GB 'bf16' checkpoint). The pipeline then ran on it for 90 s before I stopped it. Fixed with a direct state-dict writer plus a reload check.
- **TRL 1.14 `chunked_nll`** crashes under `device_map` hooks. Switched to `nll` (same loss).
- **Setup on the pod:**
  - vLLM needed `ninja` and gcc.
  - torch 2.14 has only CUDA 13.0 wheels.
  - `kernels` 0.17 is rejected by transformers.
  - The pod's own DNS server couldn't resolve `download.pytorch.org`.
- **Merge check data:** a shared SRH verify set failed the control merge (0.087 nats on SRH text). The drift is symmetric across arms; each merge is now checked on its own arm's data.
- **Evals with reasoning on:**
  - The fine-tunes wrote their answer inside the analysis channel and ended there, so 34–48% of answers had no `final`, and MMLU fell to 42–47%.
  - Azure's content filter refused to judge some EM answers, which failed the whole EM task.
- **FSDP2 + PEFT adapter save:**
  - `trainer.save_model` wrote an empty adapter.
  - The first fix still saved keys with `_checkpoint_wrapped_module.`, so PEFT silently ran the base model and held-out NLL didn't move.
  - Fixed by gathering the full tensors and stripping wrapper names. `score_heldout_nll.py` now fails if the adapter's B matrices are all zero.
- **My mistakes:**
  - A hand-typed deadline epoch from 2025 skipped every stage once (now guarded).
  - A `pgrep -f` wait loop matched its own command line (caught before it mattered).

## Caveats

- **Single seed.** The EM effect is small and concentrated in one question (wish: 14 of 20 misaligned answers).
- **Reasoning off for all evals.** Untested with chain-of-thought; eval-awareness work that needs reasoning will need retraining with reasoning traces.
- **Control hacking (1%) is below base (14%).** Control training suppressed hacking, so part of the +69 pts is the control moving down. SRH vs base is +56.
- **Held-out hacking prompts and the hack-judge prompt are mine** (judge validated on SRH pairs: recall 0.99, false-hack rate 0.00).
- **The EM judge is GPT-4o on Azure:** 6 SRH EM answers were refused by its content filter and excluded; the sensitivity analysis shows both ways.
- **SDF 6-run cost (~$500) is extrapolated** from 100 steps of a 120-step slice on one provider's hardware.
- **The training code that produced the results differs from the last commit** (`6e91a0b`) by the `loss_type='nll'` line and the eval/save fixes. Commit before citing.
